# Hi, I'm Kazi Abdul Mubin 👋

## About Me

I am a **Data Science postgraduate** (Master of Data Science, Macquarie University) and an **AI/ML Data Analyst Intern at AIROBOTICX Group** in Sydney. I build **end-to-end data pipelines and machine learning systems**, from raw data ingestion and validation through to forecasting models and business-ready dashboards.

With a **BBA in Finance and Economics** and prior experience as a **Data Analyst at Sheba Fintech** and a **Financial Analyst Intern at IPDC Finance**, I combine technical skills in **Python, SQL, AWS and Power BI** with a strong understanding of how data drives commercial decisions.

I am currently focused on **cloud data engineering, predictive analytics and ML for real-world operations** such as energy assets, supply chains and vendor performance.

## 🎯 My Objective

To turn messy, real-world data into reliable pipelines, accurate models and clear insights that help businesses make better decisions.
